Return policy

We want you to love what you ordered. If you do not, you can request a return or exchange within 7 days of delivery.

Eligible items

  • Unworn, unwashed and unaltered items
  • With original tags and packaging intact
  • Wrong, damaged or defective items received

Not eligible

  • Custom-stitched, altered or made-to-order items
  • Items without tags, or that are worn, washed or damaged after delivery
  • Innerwear, leggings used after unboxing, and items marked "Final Sale"
  • Gift boxes and accessories once opened

How to request a return

  1. Log in with your mobile number and open My Orders.
  2. Find your delivered order and tap Return.
  3. Enter the reason and submit the request.
  4. Our team reviews it and contacts you with pickup or courier details.
  5. Once the item reaches us and passes a quick check, your refund or exchange is processed.
The Return button is available for 7 days after delivery. After that, the return window is closed.

Refunds

  • Refunds go to your original payment method within 5–7 working days of approval.
  • For Cash on Delivery orders, we refund by bank transfer or UPI. We will ask for your details.
  • Original shipping charges and gift box fees are non-refundable, unless the item was wrong or defective.

Exchanges

Need a different size or colour? Raise a return request and mention the size or colour you want in the reason. Exchanges depend on stock availability. If the item is not available, we will refund you.
