Privacy Policy

How AURA collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: October 22, 2026

AURA ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, why we collect it and how we protect it.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your mobile number (used for OTP login) and name.
  • Order details: delivery address, items ordered, order value, order status and return requests.
  • Messages and reviews: information you submit through the contact form or when you write a review, including any optional photo.
  • Device data: your cart, wishlist and login status are stored in your browser (local storage) so the site works smoothly.

2. How we use your information

  • To verify your identity and let you log in securely.
  • To process, ship, track and deliver your orders.
  • To handle returns, refunds and customer support requests.
  • To improve our products, website and customer experience.
  • To send order-related updates. We send promotional messages only if you subscribe.

3. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with trusted partners who help us run the store:

  • Cloud and authentication services (such as Google Firebase) for secure login and data storage.
  • Payment partners to process your payments.
  • Courier and logistics partners to deliver your orders.
  • Authorities, when required by law.

4. Data security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your data. We never store your card or UPI PIN details. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your information.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage to remember your cart, wishlist and login state. You can clear this anytime from your browser settings, but some features may stop working.

6. Your choices

  • You can ask us to view, correct or delete your personal data.
  • You can unsubscribe from promotional messages at any time.
  • You can log out anytime from your account menu.

7. Children

Our website is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with the updated date.

9. Contact us

For any privacy questions or requests, write to us at auraclothing.vnr@gmail.com or use our contact page.
